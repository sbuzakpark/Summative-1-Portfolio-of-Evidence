 1.	Organisation Overview

Name of organisation: Mlungisi WeKhaya

Brief history of organisation 
Mlungisi WeKhaya was founded in 2015 by Sibusiso Moyo. It was initiated as a one man business, since I was skilled in fixing various things such as water supply pipes, faulty electric wires, malfunctioning toilets and geysers. Having the skills of fixing all this was enough to put food on the table. Two years later, I taught 2 workers how to drain toilets and fix leaking pipes then hired them to work for me. A year later we made enough money to build an office and a storage place where we kept our working tools. Eight years later I have taught and employed a total of 24 workers, who are currently working for me.

Mission & Vision statement
The main goal of this organization is to urgently help homes in need of fixing, repairing and building any broken structures or materials around the house. The creation of  Mlungisi WeKhaya was inspired by Matt Kaulig, founder of Leaf Home. According to (Dealroom.co, 2005), Matt started the company in 2005 out of his own basement in Ohio to offer a superior, professionally installed gutter guard system that eliminated the dangerous task of cleaning gutters. 

Target audience
The target audience that company looks forward to serve are people living in either residential areas or apartments in need of repairing and fixing of worn out and broken house equipment. The company is most especially in search of customers who have previously experienced tons of poor service.

 3.	Current Website Analysis

I performed some research on existing websites from companies that provide a similar service and found that “We Fix It All” does not include price ranges on their website for the services that they provide, according to (Wefixitallsa.co.za, 2026). Their website also does not include a historical working record (ratings) for reliability. There are no banking details to access for quick and easy payments.

4.	Proposed Website Features and Functionality.

The website will include:
•	A home page, which the client will use to navigate to other pages.
•	An about us page, which will inform the client about our brand.
•	A contact us page which will contain the company’s details.

5.	Design and User Experience

The only colours scheme that really made sense to me were colours that match with the attire and the tools we use to operate. Namely, navy, brown and silver. Each of the web pages (home, about us and contact us) will have it’s own unique theme so that the user is able to tell what page they are on. A selected font of arial rounded MT bold, with a welcoming, user friendly layout and design will be perfect for my website.

6.	Technical Requirements

For the creation of my website, I utilized Visual Studio to code and used different languages for different uses. I used HTML to define the content and structural blueprint of my webpages. I used CSS to control the visual presentation, aesthetics, and layout of the HTML elements. Finally, I used JavaScript to add logic, dynamic behaviour, and complex functionality to my website.

 7.	Timeline and Milestones

On the initial day, I attempted to build a home page, an about us page, and a contact page for my site using HTML. I encountered small difficulties as I am a novice in coding, but I eventually succeeded in adapting and learning how to develop a home page. On the third day, it became somewhat complicated when I needed to convert my basic HTML layout into an attractive and user-friendly design with CSS. I conducted research and sought help from my classmates. I acquired sufficient expertise to effectively apply CSS to its fullest potential. On the fifth day, the task became increasingly complex as I attempted to utilize JavaScript to enhance my web pages, making them interactive, dynamic, and responsive for the users. I conducted thorough research and sought advice from seasoned experts, who I immensely benefited from as I ultimately achieved the desired milestone through JavaScript.

8.	Budget
